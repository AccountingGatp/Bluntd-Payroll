Blunted Payroll Timesheet Automation

Production workflow: QBO timesheet → split Payroll / RSC cash → daily OT → approval → Gusto reconciliation → final workbook.

Production rule setJob Code 1 controls treatment. Lunch is unpaid. Rest breaks are paid and allocated to the related work stream. RSC is cash-only. Mixed RSC employees are split rather than excluded. Weekday OT starts after 8 hours; double time starts after 12. Payroll work on Saturday/Sunday is treated as overtime. Expected-output controls: Gusto Hours exclude Lunch and RSC; payroll-employee RSC is separated; CASH employees include RSC + paid Rest; job summary excludes Lunch; overtime is calculated daily.

Used only for the separate Meaghan/Ryan 14-day cycle. It must be a 14-day GL and align to the selected payroll-cycle end date.

Employee Tax / Paycheck Detail

The Basic/Detailed general ledger contains total employee-side payroll tax, but it does not reliably identify the employee-level tax split. Final special reimbursement sheets therefore use employee tax only from an explicit paycheck/tax source or from the values entered below. No employee-tax amount is guessed or hardcoded.
